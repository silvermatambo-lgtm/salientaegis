FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Useful answers. Clear expectations.

Important information about SAG services, enquiries and contracting requirements.

01 / QUESTIONS

What would you like to know?

For specific operational requirements, contact SAG directly.

Which services does SAG focus on?

Armed escorting and asset protection; infrastructure protection; executive and personal protection; static and mobile guarding; farm and rural protection; and vehicle tracking or recovery support.

Can I request a tailored risk assessment?

Yes. Use the consultation wizard or WhatsApp to describe the situation, location and preferred timeframe.

Do you protect telecommunications infrastructure?

Telecommunications towers and other critical infrastructure are identified as target markets. Engagements depend on a risk review, resources and lawful authorisation.

Do you offer security for farms and rural properties?

Farm and rural protection is an intended service category, including perimeter exposure, access management and coordinated response planning.

Can I book a high-value cargo escort?

You can submit an enquiry for assessment. Availability, route, personnel, permits and the legal requirements must be confirmed before a service is agreed.

Is SAG registered with PSiRA?

No PSiRA registration number was supplied in the company profile. Request current proof of business and personnel registration before contracting.

Are the photographs actual SAG personnel or completed projects?

No. Photographs are illustrative stock images. The website does not present proposed assignments or client relationships as confirmed work.

What happens when I use the chatbot?

It prepares an enquiry in your browser, then opens WhatsApp. You review and decide whether to send the message. No website database stores the details.
